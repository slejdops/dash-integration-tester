Interpreting Results

Failure Rate Guidelines:

  • 0%: Excellent - No issues detected
  • < 0.1%: Acceptable - Likely transient network issues
  • 0.1-1%: Concerning - Intermittent issue present
  • > 1%: Critical - Systemic LTPA or DASH issue

Response Time Guidelines:

  • P95 < 500ms: Good performance
  • P95 500-1000ms: Acceptable
  • P95 > 1000ms: Performance issue - investigate slow queries or backends
